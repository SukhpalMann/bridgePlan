The problem

Two responsibilities. One set of savings.

You may be helping your parents while building a future of your own. A large expense can put both plans in the same conversation, often before anyone knows which money is available or what a policy might cover.

BridgePlan gives you a way to explore the trade-off ahead of time. The numbers are based on scenarios you set. The unknowns become questions worth resolving.

  • Understand where a shortfall could arise
  • See the possible effect on one personal savings goal
  • Find the next policy or family question to ask
How it works

A clearer starting point for a hard conversation.

BridgePlan: Parent Shock Readiness uses a hypothetical bill, funds available quickly, and an assumed insurance payout range. It shows a possible gap and effect on one goal, then helps you find out what matters next.

01

Choose the what-if

Set the hospital-bill amount yourself. It is a scenario you want to explore, not a forecast of anyone’s care.

02

See the trade-off

Add money the family could access, an assumed payout range, and one savings goal. See where a gap could fall and how using savings might change your goal timeline.

03

Find the missing answer

Turn unclear policy terms or family contributions into specific questions for your insurer, HR, or family, in language you can use.

BridgePlan does not decide whether a claim will be paid, estimate a hospital’s deposit, or recommend an insurance policy.
